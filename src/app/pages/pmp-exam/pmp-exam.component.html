Before you begin

Exam instructions

  1. The exam has 180 questions and a 240-minute time limit. The timer runs only while you are answering questions; it pauses during breaks.
  2. Section 1 contains three case studies with 10 questions each. Read the case on the left; it stays visible while you answer.
  3. After Section 1 and Section 2 you may take an optional 10-minute break. Once you end a section, you cannot return to it.
  4. Use Flag for review to mark questions, and the section review screen to revisit unanswered or flagged items before ending a section.
  5. Right-click is not needed: use the ✕ button beside an option to strike it out, and the calculator in the top bar for project-math questions.
  6. Ten questions are unscored pretest items, as on the real exam. You will not know which ones until your results.
  7. Your progress is saved in this browser, so you can close the page and resume later.
